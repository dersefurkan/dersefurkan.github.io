Confidentiality

The private repository, the credentials, the findings, and the client’s data are the client’s. Furkan Derse uses them to do the review and to re-run the tests in the fourteen-day window.

Kept private

  • The client’s source, unless it is already public.
  • Secrets, session values, and personal data seen during the test.
  • The finding writeup, until the client publishes it or agrees in writing.

May be public

Class notes that contain no client name, no secret, and no attack text. The teaching repos already on this site. The fact that a review happened, only if the client says so.

End

Access to the staging role ends when the re-run window ends. Copies of secrets are deleted. The private repo remains the client’s.
