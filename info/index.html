Why these four, and what they are against.

The shop is one surface at a time. The menus are separate because the failure is separate. A proof can be valid and the app still pays the wrong account. A web session can be valid and the row still belongs to someone else. An agent can speak a refusal and the tool still writes.

zk

Against a valid proof bound to the wrong context. Journal fields taken from calldata. An image id that is not on the allowlist. A verifier that checks the first word of a digest and leaves the rest free. Twelve classes.

Web

Against the OWASP Top 10 (2021) categories the named flows actually touch. Access control, injection, default install, session, webhook integrity, a server that fetches a caller-chosen URL. A category outside the flows is marked not in the pack, not clean. Ten classes.

Web3

Against the identity that moves value. Two ledgers. Share inflation. A fee on the balance. Storage read from the wrong slot after an upgrade. The contract surface.

Agents

Against untrusted text that becomes a tool call. A document, a search hit from another tenant, an approval the agent minted, a resource with no caller. The sentence is not the proof. Ten classes.

What we offer

Five business days. One named surface. A private repo. A property table. A failing test or a request log when a named property breaks. One walkthrough. Fourteen days to re-run after your patch. The fee is on the sample scope, not on this page’s hero.

What we do not offer

  • An audit stamp or a guarantee.
  • A test of a system you did not hire.
  • A jailbreak list, or a run against a vendor’s public chat.
  • A hall of private findings. Client work stays in the private repo.
